Text rules of the windows

  • No paragraphs. At most one helper line of 12 words or fewer per block; longer text goes into an ⓘ popover (drawn open only in frame 2).
  • Stat tiles: label and number, a percentage where it helps. The page header says "Last 7 days" once.
  • Group headings and stat labels: sentence case, 3 words or fewer, as in the app. Column headings, buttons, menu items, and segmented choices: title-style ("Asks You", "Connect an Agent…", "Clear Filters", "All Environments").
  • Empty states: a title of 5 words or fewer, one line of 12 words or fewer, one primary button, at most one link.
  • Other Macs: one "Read-only" tag in the group or page header; "Change on <Mac>." only on the agent page.
  • Outcome words everywhere: "Allowed by bouncer", "Allowed by you", "Allowed on iPhone", "Denied by you", "Waits for you".

Help popovers (ⓘ)

  • Remote access (frame 2, open): what Funnel opens, values stay on the Mac, how a cloud agent connects, Touch ID, unlocked and awake.
  • Agent header (frame 4): kind (MCP client on the internet), connected 28 Oct with Touch ID, access valid until 26 Jan 2027, no project.
  • Never reported (frame 12a): the three steps on that Mac: open Apassy, unlock the vault, turn on Settings > Devices > Relay > "Show agents on my other devices".
