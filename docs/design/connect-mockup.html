Rules for every view

  • Prices come only from GET /v1/plans; the app has no amount in its code. Amounts show as "€3", "€3.50"; the currency comes from the plans.
  • Text follows plans section 5: the paid part is Apassy Connect, never relay sync or shared vaults; the hosted relay is never "for teams"; "free" without a limit only for what is self-hosted; the plan names are Connect Personal, Connect Family, and Connect Team.
  • Owner checks (Touch ID or the passphrase) before: confirming a device (ConfirmConnectDevice), revoking or forgetting one (RevokeConnectDevice), deleting the account (DeleteConnectAccount), every group change: confirm, remove, leave, seats (ChangeConnectGroup), sharing a vault (ShareConnectVault), accepting a shared vault (AcceptConnectShare), verifying a reset account (RepinConnectPeer), joining a shared vault (JoinConnectShare), and stopping or leaving a share (EndConnectShare). None for the plan picker, the checkout, the portal, an invite link, joining a group, or creating the account.
  • Browser: the Checkout and Portal URLs come from Connect and open in the default browser. Apassy sends Stripe nothing.
  • Locked vault: the section shows the account and the plan; every button that needs an owner check is off, with "Unlock Apassy to change Connect." under the first group.
  • Errors show as one red line with the text of ConnectError, under the group that failed, or in the sheet.
  • The iPhone shows none of this: only the plan name and its state, and "Managed in Apassy on your Mac" (I1).
