Help behind ⓘ

  • Reachable on Your Tailnet (frame 3e, open): only tailnet devices connect, nothing opens to the internet; Touch ID to switch; stops when the vault locks.
  • Node Words: "Safety words to compare: six words from this Mac's certificate." "Your iPhone shows them in Settings → Devices." "Other words there: revoke that iPhone and pair again." "The same words are not proof of this Mac. The iPhone checks the whole certificate."
  • Send Questions Through the Relay, Show Agents on My Other Devices, and the free-end line: frame 8.
  • Devices (the ⓘ by the group title): "via your Mac: straight to this Mac over your tailnet." "via Apassy relay: through apassy-relay.wyderka.cc, end-to-end encrypted." "via Apassy Connect: to this Mac through Apassy Connect, pinned to its certificate." "Tailnet: your own Linux node." "Wi-Fi: the old pairing. It ends soon."
  • Wi-Fi Pairing (frame 6c, open).

Choices made in this mockup

  • One Devices list for every device of every team. The Relay group keeps only the relay switches, the Other Macs row of a relay vault, and the free-end line (frame 8). The Wi-Fi iPhones stay in their own group until W2, because they are another pairing of the same phone.
  • Node words: six words of WORDS from the pin (bytes 0–5 of SHA-256 of the DER), monospace, no separators. The join keeps today's two safety words.
  • Port is shown in the Address, not edited. It changes only through 3g before the first pairing.
  • Revoke is a button on each row (as "Can see → Remove" in the cloud-agents mockup), not a menu.
  • The Wi-Fi notice is a once-only alert plus a permanent warning line in the group; no banner elsewhere in the app.
  • The watch checkbox in Revoke is on by default, since the watch is useless without its iPhone.

Open questions

  • Can the Mac revoke a Linux node, or only leave it? This mockup uses Details… (the L4 relay sheet) and no Revoke.
  • Does "Move Approvals" also move the other Macs that read this Mac's feed? This mockup moves only iPhones and the watch.
  • Version names in the notice: "two versions" or the version number (for example "Apassy 0.5.20")? This mockup uses words, as D9.
  • Does a watch revoke need the iPhone online? Here the Mac revokes the watch's device key directly.

Owner rules applied: no empty pane, titles of 5 words or fewer, lines of 12 words or fewer, help behind ⓘ, title case for buttons and menus (sentence case for the checkbox, as macOS), one blue button per view.
