## Outcome and constraints

At the end of stage 7 the hosted service at `apassy-relay.wyderka.cc` is **Apassy Connect**: a blind byte relay (TLS through to the user's node), a blind sync store (ciphertext and signed heads), accounts, and billing through Stripe. It holds no master key, no team vault, no item key, and no proxy route. Push stays the free gateway of D7, outside Connect. Everything Connect does can be self-hosted from this repository.

- **What Connect sees** (ADR 0037, "Who sees what"; [trust model](../security/trust-model.md)): TLS records, ciphertext, sizes, times, device names and public keys, the account's email and plan (from Stripe), vault ids (UUIDs), node names, and client addresses in memory only. Never a value, a vault, a question, or an answer. The hosted bouncer brain (ADR 0035) is the one Connect service that reads content, with its own consent screen; it is a separate process and gets only an entitlement token from Connect (section "ADR 0035" below).
- **Wire contracts stay.** relay-sync-v1 and the ADR 0019 device identity (sign-in with a P-256 key, one-use link codes, safety words, owner-confirmed joins) do not change shape. Connect adds three contracts: `connect-account-v1`, `connect-billing-v1`, `connect-tunnel-v1`.
- **No account sign-in adds a device** (plans section 3; ADR 0035 decision 6). A new device joins an account only by confirmation on an existing device with the safety words and Touch ID. There is no Apassy web sign-in in stage 7: the only web surfaces are Stripe Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal, which manage billing and never devices.
- **Prices** (owner, 2026-11-03; EUR, VAT included; a year is 10 months): Connect Personal 3 a month or 30 a year; Connect Family 5 or 50 for 4 people; Connect Team 4 or 40 for each member. **Each further Family person:** the brief of this plan listed it as the one open price; the branch head (`0edf1f90`, plans.md section 3) records the owner's decision of 1 a month or 10 a year. This plan keeps it a configuration value (`APASSY_CONNECT_FAMILY_EXTRA_CENTS`, default 100, and the yearly price with lookup key `connect_family_extra_year`, default 1000) so that a change needs no code.
- **No TEE.** Connect never does the way B swap and never holds a team key (E2 is the enterprise track). A cloud agent through Connect needs an online node of the user.
- **The free paths end with notice** (D14; plans section 4): the free questions-only Connect path of ADR 0032 and free relay sync on the hosted relay end two app releases after the release that shows the notice. Relay sync without a code (plans 4.1) ships before Connect, in the notice release.
- **Releases:** every increment ships all six components at one version (AGENTS.md). Stripe work runs in the sandbox with test clocks; no live charge in any test. The iPhone shows the plan only: no In-App Purchase, no purchase link (section "The iPhone and App Store Review Guideline 3.1").
- **Mac resources:** at most three implementation or validation workers at once on this Mac. Workers never run lint, typechecks, builds, or full suites; the top-level agent runs one pass for each revision through `~/.local/bin/heavy-check`, and prefers CI. Ops returns a plan; the VPS, Cloudflare, DNS, and Stripe live change only after the owner's confirmation.

## Decisions

Each "chosen by this plan" item is a default the owner can change.

### 1. Connect is a mode of the relay crate, not a new service

- The hosted binary stays `apassy-relay`. Two new settings: `APASSY_RELAY_CONNECT_ACCOUNTS=1` adds the account, billing, and tunnel routes to a running relay (the transition, releases R2 and R3); `APASSY_RELAY_MODE=connect` (R3 cutover) serves only Connect: accounts, vault stores (relay-sync-v1), the tunnel, Stripe, `/healthz`, and the static downloads. In `connect` mode `APASSY_RELAY_KEY` is refused, `team_vaults` is forced off, `/p/`, `/mcp`, questions, feed, push, and `POST /v1/connect` answer 410 `hosted_retired`, and no proxy is constructed. Chosen by this plan over a new crate: the sync module, the device identity, the store, the limits, the Worker, and the tests are reused as they are.
- **No master key at the end.** In `connect` mode `relay.db` and the vault-store files open without a key: plain SQLite. Nothing in them is secret (public keys, device names, account and Stripe ids, vault UUIDs, signed heads, hashes); the snapshots are SQLCipher ciphertext under the user's passphrase, which never reaches Connect. `Store` gets `open_plain`, used only in this mode. The cutover command (C5) converts the kept files with SQLite's backup API and deletes the rest. This is the literal acceptance of ADR 0037 stage 7 ("no master key"). The trust model lists the plain files.
- **Directory 1007** (reserved for this plan): `account`, `account_device`, `account_link`, `vault_store` (a `team` row of kind `account` plus `team.account_id`), `group`, `group_member`, `group_invite`, `stripe_customer`, `stripe_subscription`, `stripe_event`, `tunnel_grant`, `entitlement_key`. Additive; the images before refuse 1007 as they refuse 1003 today (rollback = the backup of the deploy). The other parallel plans use 1004 to 1006 (not this plan's concern; the migration chain must be linear, so C1 rebases on whatever lands first and renumbers its own step to the next free number if 1007 is no longer the next).

### 2. Accounts and devices

- **Account** `acc_<10 base32>`: one person (Personal), one organizer with members (Family), or a company (Team). A member of a Family or Team has an account of their own with `group_id`. Creation is free and needs no plan: `POST /v1/account` with a device key and a signature of `"apassy-connect account v1" 0x00 <origin> 0x00 <public key b64u>` (the form of `connect_message`, its own domain). The first device is the Mac that creates it. The account has no password and no email of its own; the email lives at Stripe (the checkout collects it) and Connect copies it after `checkout.session.completed` for the Devices list and support. No marketing, no mail from Connect in stage 7.
- **Account device key**: one P-256 key for each physical device, kept outside every vault: on the Mac a Keychain item through `NativeHelper` (this device only, non-synchronizable, the pattern of `src/node/master_key.rs`); on the iPhone the Keychain of `ApassyCompanionKit`; on Linux a 0600 file (stage 3). Sign-in: `POST /v1/account/challenge` and `POST /v1/account/token` with the message `"apassy-connect sign-in v1" 0x00 <origin> 0x00 <account id> 0x00 <public key b64u> 0x00 <nonce>`; a 15-minute token in memory. The bouncer brain service (ADR 0035 H) signs in with the same key and its own domain tag.
- **Adding a device** is the relay-sync-v1 section 7 flow at the account level: an existing device makes a one-use 10-minute link (`POST /v1/account/devices/link`), the new device joins with its key (`POST /v1/account/devices/join`, edge only), both show the safety words derived from both keys (the ADR 0019 words), and the existing device confirms with a fresh owner check (`OwnerAction::ConfirmConnectDevice`, Touch ID). Revoke: `DELETE /v1/account/devices/{id}` from any live device with an owner check. A revoked device loses its account token at once and is removed from every vault store. Chosen by this plan: the app reuses `src/sync/relay/devices.rs` for the words and the sheet.
- **Vault stores**: one relay-sync-v1 team for each vault, `POST /v1/account/vaults {vault_id}` by an account device → a team of kind `account` with `TeamLimits::account(plan)` (`sync_bytes` 64 MiB, 10 devices for each member, no items, no agents, no questions) and the creating device as device 1. Other account devices are added without a link: `POST /v1/account/vaults/{team}/devices {device_id}` (a device of the same account, or of a Family or Team member account for a shared vault, from a device that is already in the store; a shared vault still needs the passphrase on the joining device and goes through the "Add a Mac" merge of relay-sync-v1 section 6 and 7 for the content). The relay-sync-v1 endpoints then work unchanged: team sign-in with `<team id>/<device id>`, signed heads, chain, receipts, `DELETE /v1/sync`. The head grammar does not change. The plan's limit for vault stores: Personal 10, Family 10 for each person, Team 10 for each member (chosen; the owner can change it).
- **Recovery** when every device is lost: the account creation shows a one-time recovery code (16 words, shown once on the Mac, "Save it in another password manager"); `POST /v1/account/recover {code, device}` works only when the account has no live device, makes the new device the first device, and deletes every vault store (a new device set cannot verify the old chains) while the subscription continues. Chosen by this plan.
- **Entitlement token** for other Connect services (the bouncer brain of ADR 0035): `GET /v1/account/entitlement` → `{"token": "<b64u(json)>.<b64u(ed25519 sig)>"}` with claims `{v:1, acc, plan, group, feats:["brain","tunnel","sync"], iat, exp (24 h)}`, signed with the Connect signing key (`APASSY_CONNECT_SIGNING_KEY_FILE`, Ed25519, ring). The public key is at `GET /.well-known/apassy-connect.json`. A service checks the signature and `exp`; it never calls Connect at request time (ADR 0035 section 5: separate processes).

### 3. Billing: Stripe Checkout and the Customer Portal, no SDK

- **Products and prices** (sandbox first, live after the owner's confirmation): rename "Personal EUR 1" → "Connect Personal" and "Team EUR 2 per member" → "Connect Team", add "Connect Family"; archive the old prices. New prices, currency EUR, `tax_behavior=inclusive`, with lookup keys: `connect_personal_month` 300, `connect_personal_year` 3000; `connect_family_month` and `connect_family_year` as graduated tiers (tier 1 up to 4 people: `flat_amount` 500 or 5000; tier 2 and up: `unit_amount` = the Family extra, default 100 or 1000); `connect_team_month` 400 and `connect_team_year` 4000 per seat. Connect resolves prices by lookup key at start (`GET /v1/prices?lookup_keys[]=…&active=true`) and caches them; no price id in code or config. The yearly price is a price of its own, not a coupon. The script `crates/apassy-relay/scripts/stripe-products.sh` makes them with the Stripe CLI, idempotently by lookup key, and refuses `--live` unless `CONFIRM_LIVE=yes`.
- **Checkout**: `POST /v1/account/checkout {plan, interval, people|seats}` (account device) → Connect creates a Checkout Session (`mode=subscription`, the price by lookup key, `quantity` = people or seats, `automatic_tax[enabled]=true`, `billing_address_collection=required`, `customer_update[address]=auto`, `tax_id_collection[enabled]=true` for Team, `client_reference_id=<account id>`, `metadata[account_id]`, `consent_collection[terms_of_service]=required`, `success_url=https://apassy.wyderka.cc/connect/done?session_id={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}`, `cancel_url=https://apassy.wyderka.cc/connect/cancel`) and answers `{url, session_id}`. The Mac opens the URL in the default browser. The app learns the result by polling `GET /v1/account` every 3 s while the sheet is open (the webhook sets the plan), and the done page also offers "Return to Apassy" (`apassy://connect/done?session=<id>`; the app registers the `apassy` URL scheme if it does not have one yet: see "Not verified"). The page never carries a secret.
- **Portal**: `POST /v1/account/portal` → a Customer Portal session URL; the portal (configured by the owner) handles the payment method, invoices, the cancel at period end, and the switch between Personal and Family (`subscription_update` with the Connect products). People and seats change from the app (`POST /v1/account/group/seats`), because membership changes drive them: Connect updates the subscription item quantity with `proration_behavior=create_prorations`.
- **Webhook**: `POST /v1/stripe/webhook`, through the Worker (body cap 256 KiB on this exact path, no per-address limiter: Stripe sends from its own addresses), checked with `Stripe-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of `<t>.<body>` with the endpoint secret, 5-minute tolerance, constant-time compare with `ring::hmac::verify`), idempotent by event id (`stripe_event`). Events: `checkout.session.completed` (binds the customer and subscription to the account from `client_reference_id`), `customer.subscription.created|updated|deleted` (status, items, quantity, `current_period_end`, `cancel_at_period_end`), `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`. The relay acts on the webhook, never on the browser's return.
- **Plan state** (`stripe_subscription.status`): `active` or `trialing` = entitled; `past_due` = entitled for 14 days of grace (Stripe's Smart Retries run meanwhile), then read-only; `canceled`, `unpaid`, `incomplete_expired` = read-only for 30 days (pulls and downloads work, pushes get 403 `plan_required`, the tunnel refuses dials with 403 `plan_required`), and the vault stores are deleted 90 days after the end with a notice in the app from day 1. Account deletion in the app (owner check): the subscription is canceled at once (`DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{id}`), every store and device row is deleted, the Stripe customer is kept for the invoices (legal retention). Chosen by this plan.
- **Groups**: `group(kind family|team, owner_account, subscription)`; Family = organizer + members, included 4 people, each further person at the extra price; Team = owner, admins, members, one seat each. Invite: `POST /v1/account/group/invites` → `https://apassy.wyderka.cc/connect/join#<code>` (one use, 7 days); the invited person pastes it on their Mac (Settings › Connect › Join a family or team), their account joins as `pending`; the organizer or an admin sees the pending member's name and confirms with an owner check; the quantity update follows. Remove: `DELETE /v1/account/group/members/{account}` (owner or admin; a removed member keeps a free account and loses the entitlement, their devices are removed from the group's shared stores). Seats below the members count are refused (`seat_limit`).
- **VAT-inclusive**: the customer always pays the listed amount; Stripe Tax computes the VAT part for the customer's country; the net differs by country. Registrations are owner-needed (below).

### 4. The byte relay (tunnel) and the cloud-agent ingress

- **Node side**: a node (the Mac app, later the Linux node) keeps one outbound WebSocket to Connect, `GET /v1/tunnel/node` with the account token, `Upgrade: websocket`, subprotocol `apassy-tunnel-v1`. Binary frames multiplex streams: `[stream u32 BE][kind u8][flags u8][len u16][payload]`; `kind` is `1` device (a dial by a device of the account or of a granted account) or `2` public (a cloud agent through the ingress); flags `open`, `close`, `reset`. The node feeds each stream into its TLS front (2B) as if it were an accepted socket: for `device` it serves the pinned node certificate and the node listener; for `public` it serves the public certificate (below) and the public listener (`Scope::Public`, `/mcp` and `/p/`). So TLS ends on the node and Connect sees TLS records. A node registers its id (`node_<id>`, stable, from the account) and its certificate pin; a second connection of the same node replaces the first.
- **Device side** (iPhone, another Mac, a Linux node): `GET /v1/tunnel/dial/{node}` with the account token, `Upgrade: websocket`; after 101 the WebSocket is a byte pipe to the node's TLS front; the client runs its pinned TLS (2C) inside it. The pairing QR (2E) adds `alt=wss://apassy-relay.wyderka.cc/v1/tunnel/dial/<node>` beside the tailnet address, so a device tries the tailnet first and Connect second. A Family or Team member's device may dial a node of another account only with a `tunnel_grant` (made by the node's account when it invites the member's account); this is what a team host on a node needs across tailnets without Tailscale (ADR 0037 section 9, "No Tailscale").
- **Cloud agents** (ADR 0037 stage 7 acceptance: "a cloud agent through Connect"): a cloud agent speaks plain HTTPS to a URL, so Connect gives each node a public name `<node>.node.apassy.wyderka.cc` (the zone is owner-needed; chosen name) and an **SNI ingress**: a TCP listener on the VPS (`APASSY_CONNECT_INGRESS_LISTEN=0.0.0.0:443`, DNS-only wildcard record, not through the Cloudflare Worker, which is HTTP only) that reads the server name from the ClientHello, forwards the raw TLS stream through the node's tunnel as a `public` stream, and writes nothing else. The node holds a publicly trusted certificate for its name: it gets it with ACME (Let's Encrypt, HTTP-01) through the same tunnel: Connect forwards `http://<node name>/.well-known/acme-challenge/*` (port 80 of the ingress) to the node as a `public` plain stream, and the node answers its own challenge; the key never leaves the node. This mirrors Tailscale Funnel. The URL for the agent is `https://<node>.node.apassy.wyderka.cc/mcp`, next to the Funnel URL in Settings › Cloud agents.
  Fallback F1 (the owner can choose it to ship sooner): Cloudflare ends the agent's TLS at the Worker, and Connect forwards the agent's HTTP to the node inside the pinned TLS of a `device` stream. Then Cloudflare and Connect see the agent's requests (bearer token, placeholders, session keys), never values. This is a privacy downgrade against "Connect sees only TLS records" and must be written into the trust model if chosen. Chosen by this plan: the SNI ingress.
- **Limits** (chosen): for each account 64 open streams, 8 nodes, dial rate 60 a minute, a soft byte budget of 20 GiB a month (Family: for each person; Team: for each member), over it 429 `tunnel_limit` until the next month; WebSocket pings every 30 s (Cloudflare closes idle WebSockets), reconnect with backoff; no stream content is logged or stored; the audit holds counts and bytes per account.
- **Push** for questions that the iPhone fetches through the tunnel: the node asks the push gateway of 2F with the iPhone's push handle (D7, free). Stage 7 needs 2F; until 2F ships, a question through the tunnel reaches the iPhone when the app is open (long poll through the tunnel) and has no push. The Worker's `/internal/push` route and the relay's push thread are removed at the cutover (C5), after 2F is live.

### 6. The iPhone and App Store Review Guideline 3.1

The iPhone app is free and a companion to a paid web-based service, so it relies on guideline **3.1.3(f)** ("Free Stand-alone Apps": free apps acting as a stand-alone companion to a paid web-based tool do not need to use In-App Purchase, provided there is no purchasing inside the app and no calls to action for purchase outside of the app) and **3.1.3(b)** ("Multiplatform Services": users may use what they bought elsewhere). Guideline 3.1.1 forbids buttons, external links, or other calls to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms other than IAP, except as 3.1.3 allows. The US storefront link entitlement (after the 2025 court order) and the EU alternative terms allow link-outs under their own rules; this plan does not use either (chosen: one iPhone app, no purchase surface).

| The iPhone app may | The iPhone app must not |
| --- | --- |
| Show the plan of the account it belongs to: "Apassy Connect · Connect Personal · renews 3 Dec 2026", "Connect Family · 3 of 4 people", "Not on Connect", "Past due", "Ends 12 Jan 2027" | Show a price, a currency, "from 3 a month", a comparison of plans, or a trial |
| Say where it is managed: "Managed in Apassy on your Mac" (text, no link) | Show "Buy", "Subscribe", "Upgrade", "Renew", "Manage subscription", or any button that leads to the checkout or the portal |
| Use everything the account gives (sync store reads, the tunnel), as 3.1.3(b) allows | Link to `apassy.wyderka.cc/pricing`, `/connect/*`, the Stripe portal, or say "subscribe on our website" |
| Join an account as a device by confirmation on the Mac, and show the safety words | Create an account, start a checkout, or collect an email |
| Work fully without Connect: Wi-Fi, Tailscale (2G), iCloud sync, local approvals | Degrade a free function or show an upsell when Connect is absent |
| Mention Apassy Connect in App Store metadata as a feature of the Mac app ("works with Apassy Connect") | Name prices or purchase steps in the iOS listing, screenshots, or release notes |

Apple reads "e.g." in 3.1.3(f) narrowly at times (forum reports; see "Not verified"), so the App Review notes of the first build with the plan row explain: the Mac app is the product, the iPhone app is its free companion, there is no purchase or purchase link in the iPhone app, and the plan row is informational. The App Privacy answers add the account id as an identifier linked to the user (owner-needed).

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| WebSocket upgrades through the Cloudflare Worker and the Workers VPC binding to the relay are not verified (the Worker forwards HTTP today). | C4 starts with a one-day spike on a staging route; if the VPC binding does not carry WebSockets, the tunnel routes go through a Cloudflare Tunnel public hostname for the Worker or directly to the VPS behind the ingress listener (then also over the ingress port with its own TLS). |
| Cloudflare closes idle WebSockets and limits their life; a long-lived node tunnel reconnects often. | Pings every 30 s, reconnect with backoff, streams end cleanly; the node shows "Connect: reconnecting" and the iPhone retries the dial. |
| Let's Encrypt limits: 50 new certificates a week for one registered domain (`wyderka.cc`), so at most 50 new nodes a week under `*.node.apassy.wyderka.cc`, and the site and relay certificates count too. | The owner can buy a dedicated registered domain for node names (step 4); the limit is then 50 nodes a week for it alone; the plan's renewal at 30 days left keeps renewals spread; a node that cannot get a certificate still serves devices (pinned) and shows the reason. |
| The ingress exposes the VPS address and a raw TCP port to the internet (the API stays behind Cloudflare). | The ingress parses only the server name with a cap and a deadline, forwards only to registered nodes, rate-limits by address in memory; a separate small ingress host or a Hetzner floating address is the next step if abuse appears; the Worker path does not change. |
| Stage 2 (node, TLS front, pinned trust, pairing, push gateway) is on the critical path of M4, M5, I2. | R2 can ship accounts, billing, the sync store, and the plan row without the tunnel; the tunnel joins R3 and the cutover moves one release, with the notice date updated. |
| Money paths: a forged webhook or a seat mismatch gives free service or a wrong charge. | Signature and replay checks with vectors, state only from webhooks, quantity only through Connect, the sandbox test-clock job, the judge panel on C2 and C3. |
| Apple reads 3.1.3(f) narrowly and rejects the plan row. | The row is text only; the review notes explain; the fallback is a build without the row (the iPhone then says nothing about the plan), which changes no function. |
| Plain files at rest on the VPS hold device names, public keys, emails, and vault ids. | Stated in the trust model and the privacy policy; the files hold no secret; disk encryption of the VPS is the owner's choice; backups go through the existing encrypted pull. |
| Two new vault schema bumps in parallel plans (32 and 34). | Schema 34 is local-only and additive; M1 rebases on the merged 32 (and 33 if any) and keeps the linear chain; the down-migration is documented. |
| The `apassy://` URL scheme may not exist in the app bundle. | Polling is the primary path; the scheme is a convenience; `scripts/build-app.sh` adds `CFBundleURLTypes` if absent (see "Not verified"). |
| Cloud-agent traffic through the tunnel (way B requests and answers of the agent) costs bandwidth on the VPS. | The byte budget per account, counted in C4; the owner can lower it; the Funnel route stays the free path for users with Tailscale. |
