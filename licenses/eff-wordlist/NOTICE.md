# EFF Long Wordlist

The memorable passwords of the iPhone app (`ios/ApassyCore/data/eff_large_wordlist.txt`) use the words of the EFF Long Wordlist, without its dice numbers.

- Source: https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt (SHA-256 `addd35536511597a02fa0a9ff1e5284677b8883b83e986e43f15a3db996b903e`), described at https://www.eff.org/dice.
- Author: the Electronic Frontier Foundation (Joseph Bonneau and others), 2016.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/.
- Change: the dice numbers of each line are removed. The 7776 words are unchanged and in their order.
